EPIC Junior II

3 - 6 August 2026

2nd School of Scientific Computing for High School Students

What is EPIC Junior?

For the second time, the EPIC series of schools offers a space for high school students across our country to learn about scientific computing and interact with Ecuadorian scientists.

High school students who take part in EPIC Junior will have the great opportunity to:

  • Attend scientific talks prepared by our invited speakers
  • Take part in scientific programming mini-workshops prepared by university students
  • Learn about science and technology in an interactive environment

  • Write a strong motivation letter in the registration form
