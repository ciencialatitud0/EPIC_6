EPIC VI - Computational physics meets AI: From Data to models in physical and life sciences


The Physics Without Frontiers (PWF) programme of the the Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP) and the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO), KHIPUx Latin American events in AI, in collaboration with Grupo de Investigadores Latitud Cero, Universidad Yachay Tech, Universidad Internacional del Ecuador (UIDE), Universidad de la Américas (UDLA) and Universidad San Francisco de Quito (USFQ) have the pleasure to invite students to participate in the EPIC VI School.

The EPIC School is a scientific programming contest aimed at motivating BSc and Master students in Ecuador to pursue careers that combine computer science with natural sciences (physics, in particular). Students who participate in this year's edition will have the opportunity to team up with a peer and together demonstrate their Python coding abilities to solve tricky problems related to scientific computing.

General Information

The 6th edition of EPIC will focus on introducing computational techniques for the analysis of scientific data and images by using programming languages such as Python, MATLAB, and Mathematica as tools. Instructors will present real research problems to illustrate the use of computational tools in the experimental or theoretical study of physical systems. Basic programming notions are essential to sign up for this workshop.

The tutorials will focus on the following areas:

  • Data and image analysis
  • Machine learning
  • Astrophysics and cosmology
  • Biophysics
  • Particle physics
  • Materials science
  • Fluid dynamics

EPIC 6 is kindly supported and funded by ICTP’s Physics Without Frontiers (PWF) program and UNESCO-Quito. If you would like to know more about the PWF team, please have a look at their ongoing and previous projects: Physics Without Frontiers (PWF) Program.

Students and mentors from EPIC V come together during one of the school's hands-on sessions, reflecting the collaborative, hands-on spirit that defines every edition of EPIC. You can revisit the full EPIC V programme, photos, and highlights on its dedicated website: ciencialatitud0.github.io/EPIC_5.

Format

Pre-School Online Engagement

EPIC VI will include a series of open online preparatory sessions designed to introduce participants to basic programming concepts, computational thinking, and the overall structure of the school.

The pre-school activities are designed to:

  • Reach students from universities across Ecuador
  • Harmonize heterogeneous academic backgrounds
  • Identify motivated participants without competitive selection stages

In-Person School (5 Days)

The in-person school combines conceptual lectures with guided hands-on workshops using Python-based tools.

Each day includes:

  • One conceptual lecture introducing key scientific ideas
  • Guided practical workshops using Python
  • Emphasis on models, assumptions, and scientific interpretation

The tutorials will focus on:

  • Computational modeling of physical systems
  • Numerical simulations and data analysis
  • Machine learning for physical and biological data
  • Physics-inspired and interpretable AI approaches

All activities are designed to be accessible to advanced undergraduate students, with no prior experience in machine learning required.

Collaborative Group Activity

The final day replaces the hackathon format used in previous editions with a guided collaborative modeling activity.

Participants will:

  • Work in small groups on clearly defined modeling tasks
  • Apply methods learned during the school
  • Focus on conceptual understanding and teamwork
  • Present short summaries of their approach and results

Objectives

  • Introduce advanced undergraduate students to computational modeling and simulations
  • Provide hands-on experience with data analysis and introductory AI methods
  • Strengthen the connection between physical intuition and computational tools
  • Prepare students for thesis projects, graduate studies, or research internships
  • Establish EPIC as a stable national training school with international links

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply to EPIC VI?

+

We welcome applications from undergraduate students, MSc students and PhD students working on any field of natural sciences and/or computer sciences who wish to expand their scientific computing skills. While we review all applications, preference is given to applicants from Latin American countries and to students who have not participated in previous editions of EPIC.

Do I need programming experience?

+

Basic programming notions are essential to sign up for this School. Familiarity with Python is recommended as it will be the primary language used throughout the competition.

Is there a registration fee?

+

No, there is no registration fee for EPIC VI. The event is fully funded by ICTP's Physics Without Frontiers (PWF) program and UNESCO-Quito.

Can I participate remotely?

+

No, the EPIC VI School will be held in person at Universidad Internacional del Ecuador (UIDE) in Quito.

Goals and Deliverables

EPIC VI aims to strengthen computational science education in Ecuador by providing advanced undergraduate and early Master’s students with hands-on experience in computational modeling, simulations, data analysis, and introductory artificial intelligence methods.

The school combines conceptual lectures with guided Python-based workshops, emphasizing the connection between physical intuition and computational tools. Activities are designed to prioritize scientific reasoning, interpretation, and teamwork.

By the end of EPIC VI, participants will:

  • Gain practical experience in computational modeling and simulations
  • Understand how data-driven and model-based approaches complement each other
  • Develop confidence in applying computational tools to scientific problems
  • Improve teamwork and scientific communication skills

All teaching materials, including lecture notes, slides, Python notebooks, and hands-on tutorials, will be released as open-access educational resources.

EPIC VI aims to leave participants with not only enhanced technical skills but also a deeper understanding of how computational methods drive innovation in diverse scientific fields.
